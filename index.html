MY STORY

It started with writing code.

The part of engineering that has stayed with me is simple: understanding a problem well enough to build something useful.

My path has taken me from software engineering to leading technology and platform teams at companies including Amazon, TeleTracking, and now Veritext. The industries have changed, from advertising to healthcare to legal services. The work has always brought me back to people and the problems they’re trying to solve.

I believe I’m a better leader when I stay close to the work: listening to the people who use our software, building alongside engineers, and asking where we can make things simpler. AI is opening up new possibilities for that kind of hands-on work, and it’s a big part of what I’m exploring now.

MY APPROACH

How I like to work.

01

Stay close to the build

Architecture matters to me because of what it makes possible for the people building and using the software. I like turning complex systems into something easier to understand, change, and support.

⌘
02

Learn by making things

I’m exploring AI by using it to build. Getting an idea into someone’s hands, hearing what works, and improving it teaches me more than a slide deck ever could.

03

Give people ownership

I want engineers to understand why their work matters and have the room to own it. Clear expectations, direct conversations, and shared responsibility are central to how I lead.
